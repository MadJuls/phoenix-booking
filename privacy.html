Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Phoenix Auto Details LLC ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website phoenix-auto-details.com and use our mobile detailing booking services.

Information We Collect

When you book an appointment on our website, we collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address / location for mobile detailing
  • Vehicle details (make, model, year)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and fulfill your mobile detailing appointments
  • Send SMS and email confirmations, updates, and reminders related to your service
  • Communicate regarding customer support or service inquiries
SMS Consent & Privacy Protection:
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. All personal information and phone numbers collected for text messaging are strictly used to communicate with you regarding your service appointments with Phoenix Auto Details LLC.

Opting Out of SMS Communications

You can cancel SMS notifications at any time. Simply reply STOP to any message received from us. After you send "STOP", we will send you a confirmation message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. For help, reply HELP or contact us directly.
